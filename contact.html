FAQ
How do I cancel a booking?+
You can cancel your booking up to 48 hours before the event from My Tickets page. Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days.
Where is my e-ticket?+
Your e-ticket is sent to your registered email immediately after booking. You can also find it under My Tickets in your dashboard.
Can I transfer my ticket?+
Tickets are non-transferable by default. Contact support if you need assistance.
How do I become an organizer?+
Email us at organizers@eventhub.in with your event details and we'll get you set up.
